The Women Who Helped Bring the Nazis to Justice

 

Inside the Trial of the Century

In November 1945, the world looked to Nuremberg for the truth about Nazi crimes and a vision of justice. But behind this defining moment in history were extraordinary women whose stories have remained largely untold.

In The Nuremberg Women, bestselling author and historian Natalie Livingstone tells the story of the trial through eight women who were there: a brilliant American lawyer, three pioneering journalists (two German and one British), an iconoclastic artist, a refugee aristocrat, an Auschwitz survivor and a young Russian translator.

Full of drama, courage and astonishing personal stories, it’s impossible to put down.
